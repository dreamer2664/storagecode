## One thing I would tell my own successor

I broke a repo twice by writing tests that wrote into the evidence directory: 282
synthetic frames landed in `results/bot_shots/` and a re-learned `templates.npz`
overwrote a good one. Both were cleaned (`git clean -fdq results/bot_shots/`,
`git checkout results/templates.npz`) and tests now write to a temp `SABLE_SHOTS_DIR`
and a private templates copy. **A test suite that can corrupt the evidence it is
supposed to protect is worse than no suite**, because you stop trusting the data and
therefore stop trusting the tests.

# Video-making training campaign

**Director decision — 15 October 2026.** Mars is paused. The active priority is training agents to make real, good five-minute YouTube videos. Subjects are chosen by the agents; all ten use English narration and captions.

## The output, not just the paperwork

- At least **10 distinct, accepted final MP4s**.
- **300–330 seconds each**, at least **50 minutes total**; no silent/static/loop padding.
- 1080p picture, intelligible narration, synchronized SRT/VTT captions, thumbnails and upload-ready metadata.
- Source/rights ledgers, complete rough cut, independent critique, revised final.
- **Two distinct non-owner full-watch reviews**, bound to the final SHA-256; each ≥80/100 with no major unresolved defect.
- V15 stays blocked until all ten real files can be retrieved and screened.

## 3. Production method (rerunnable)

- `narration.json` — 14 narrative segments, 66 sentences; `tts_chunks` = the 5 recorded clips.
- `assets/voice/c01..c05.mp3` — narration takes (318.37 s total).
- `build/master.wav` — assembly (0.45 s inter-clip silence; 320.87 s).
- `build/sentence_times.json` — sentence timings: proportional-to-character placement within each clip, **snapped to real silence gaps** (`build/gaps.txt`, silencedetect −35 dB/0.28 s, 132 gaps).
- `make_assets.py` — corpus regeneration (clean `git archive main` checkout), charts, cards, 3 animations (38 frames).
- `build_video.py` — 35-shot table (33 stills/animations → clips), concat, captions from the same timings (SRT/VTT/transcript, ≤42 chars/line, 2 lines), 2-pass loudnorm to −16 LUFS / TP −1.5.
- `build/timeline.json` — per-shot timecodes + final sha256.
- `build/loudnorm_measure.json` — two-pass loudnorm measurements (shared with the assembled file, so the shipped bytes and the measurement come from the same source).
